🔥 4 Golden Rules to Crack Interview Boards

1. Master the STAR Formula

For situational questions, structure answers using Situation → Task → Action → Result with measurable numbers.

2. Research the Company First

Know the company's latest product, competitors, and mission before walking in—recruiters love prepared candidates.

3. Own Your CV Projects

Be ready to explain every single line, GPA, project, or skill listed on your CV in under 60 seconds clearly.

4. Ask a Smart Closing Question

When asked "Do you have any questions?", ask about team KPIs or growth paths in the first 90 days.
